TITLE
coi-lint — linting for certificates of insurance

DESCRIPTION
Checking insurance certificates by eye? coi-lint checks a COI against your project's requirements in one command (holder, limits, additional insured, waiver of subrogation, expiry) and drafts the deficiency letter for you. Offline Python CLI, MIT licensed; PDF input is next on the roadmap.

Code: https://github.com/AagamS06/coi-lint
Independent student project; not affiliated with any company mentioned.
